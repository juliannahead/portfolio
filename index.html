    I'm Julianna Head, a Digital Operations & Growth Architect. I work across design, engineering, marketing, product, and data, and I'm usually the one who sees how they connect.

    My clients include wellness and hospitality companies, professional services firms, consumer and DTC brands, and growth-stage companies. For them I've built internal web apps, automated client systems, CRMs, and VoIP/IVR phone systems, run paid ads, and managed a consumer mobile app.

    I start by finding where revenue leaks out of a funnel or an operation. Then I design the fix, build it, market it, measure it, and keep improving it. One person doing all of it means nothing gets lost in a handoff.
