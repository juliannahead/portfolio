A2Pea Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

A2Pea ("the extension") is published by Julianna Head ("we"). This policy explains what the extension does with data.

What the extension reads

When you click Run A2P audit, the extension reads the web page open in that tab, plus that site's privacy policy and terms pages, to check them for SMS compliance. If you allow it, the extension also reads policy pages hosted on another domain. It only reads pages when you click.

What stays on your device

Everything the audit reads is analysed inside your browser. Page content, audit results, the brand name and template details you enter are stored only in your browser's extension storage. We do not receive them.

What is sent anywhere

Nothing. The extension has no accounts, no payments, no analytics, no tracking and no ads, and we don't collect browsing history.

Sharing

We don't sell or share any data. We don't receive any data to share.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the extension to delete everything it has stored.

Children

The extension is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.

Contact

Use the contact form on juliannahead.com.
